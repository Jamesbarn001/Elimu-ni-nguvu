## Structure Summary (Post-PWPER Guidelines)
* **Total Examinable Subjects Per Student:** Exactly **7 subjects** (4 Compulsory Core + 3 Pathway Electives).
* **Compulsory Non-Examinable:** Physical Education (PE), Digital Literacy / ICT Skills, Pastoral Programme of Instruction (PPI).
* **National Assessment:** Kenya Senior School Education Assessment (KSSEA) via cumulative formula (15% Grade 10 SBA + 15% Grade 11 SBA + 70% Grade 12 KSSEA), awarding the Kenya Certificate of Basic Education (KCBE).
